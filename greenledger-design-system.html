Foundations for climate integrity

Clarity for every carbon claim.

A trusted, calm interface language for GreenLedger—designed to help project teams, verifiers, and beneficiaries make high-consequence decisions with confidence.

Our north star

Evidence should feel inspectable.

Never hide a risk state behind a decorative interface. Make sources, ownership, and lifecycle status understandable at a glance.

PreciseInformation with provenance.
CalmSerious, never alarmist.
HumanDecisions stay with people.
